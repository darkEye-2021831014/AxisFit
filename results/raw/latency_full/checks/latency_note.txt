All latencies in this folder: single window, batch size 1, 1 CPU thread, median of 900 timed calls per unit (IQR, 5th, 95th and 99th percentile also reported), preparation (slice, features or z-score) plus inference, measured in one session on Intel(R) Xeon(R) CPU @ 2.00GHz (4 usable logical CPUs). One model per unit, trained on the training subjects of fold 0. The numbers include the call overhead of scikit-learn, XGBoost and PyTorch. They are NOT edge-device latencies and NOT energy. Duty cycle = latency / 2000 ms hop = share of one core of this CPU. Data volume is an analytic model of raw sample values only (16-bit and 32-bit), without headers, timestamps, transport overhead or compression.
